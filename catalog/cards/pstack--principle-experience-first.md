Product-flavoured on its face - say no to a thousand things, ship less and ship better, prototype before committing, sweat the details, tighten the core loop.

What makes it transferable to non-product work is the definition of *user*: whoever consumes the work. For a UI that is the end user; for a library or internal API it is the colleague who imports it; **the engineer who maintains the code next is a user too**, whose experience is weighed the same way, with impact explained from their seat. That reframing turns a product principle into an interface-design and maintainability one.

It also draws a clean line against its neighbour: foundational thinking governs the *sequence* of work, this governs the *target*. Foundations serve the experience, not the reverse.
