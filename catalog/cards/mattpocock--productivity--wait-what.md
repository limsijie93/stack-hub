Two sentences long. Invoked mid-conversation when a message did not land: re-pitch it with context, in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, using the project's ubiquitous language.

Worth stealing for two reasons out of proportion to its size. Naming a **specific controlled-language standard** rather than saying 'plain English' gives the instruction real teeth - STE100 has actual rules about vocabulary and sentence length. And it is the clearest example in either stack of a skill that is one corrective sentence, usable *inside* any other skill without leaving it. It is the after-the-fact cure; agreeing shared language up front is the preventative.
