One sentence: restate your last message, stop using jargon, speak coherently and concisely, like one human talking to another.

The near-duplicate of mattpocock's wait-what, and the comparison is instructive. Both are one-line correctives for a message that did not land. wait-what names a specific controlled-language standard (ASD-STE100) and pulls in the project glossary; bro just says 'like one human talking to another'. wait-what is the stronger instruction because it recruits a real standard rather than a vibe - which is that stack's own leading-word principle applied to itself. Worth carding mainly as evidence that the same micro-concept appears independently in two stacks, and that the better version is the one with a concrete referent behind the adjective.
