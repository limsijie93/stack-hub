Thirty-one numbered AI tells grouped into content, language, style, communication artifacts, filler, jargon and plain speech, each naming the pattern and the fix. Concrete enough to act on: em dashes banned outright with the note that reaching for parentheses instead just trades one tell for another; a list of abstract metaphor nouns (substrate, wedge, vector, nexus, primitive-as-noun, surface, flywheel) each mapped to a plainer concrete word; inline-header lists distinguished carefully from legitimate bold lead-ins.

Two things lift it above a style guide. **Removing patterns is only half the job** - sterile voiceless writing is equally obvious - so it gives positive instructions: have opinions, vary rhythm, acknowledge complexity, let some mess in. And rule 27, **say what it does, not how it feels**, with a test that generalises far beyond prose: if you cannot restate the sentence as a concrete instruction, fact or number, cut it; and if the sentence could appear unchanged in another project's docs, it says nothing about this one, so cut it. The final self-audit step - ask 'what makes this obviously AI generated?' and fix what remains - is a cheap and effective closing move.
